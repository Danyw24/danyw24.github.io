Profile

Informatics student at FIT ČVUT in Prague. I build and test systems around AI agents: I connect them to real services through APIs and the Model Context Protocol (MCP), and I study how they break. I verify before I trust anything, and I write down what I find.

Selected work

Security research

  • mcp-radar — watches 18 MCP servers and SDKs for silent security patches. It flagged three fixes before their public advisory, by 8 hours, 25 hours and 9.5 days.
  • MCP tool poisoning lab — a proof of concept where a poisoned tool description makes an agent leak a fake secret without the user noticing, plus a credited reproduction of CVE-2025-68143.
  • The php2shell recipe — research lab on the disclosed CVE-2026-109618/19: the public chain verified under gdb on three PHP builds, and the method to map which versions are affected.
  • ZyXEL P-870HN, from bare PCB to root — hardware hacking on my own router: board identification, serial console, and a reproduction of CVE-2025-0890 and CVE-2024-40891.
  • CTF — hack.lu CTF 2026: solved Talk to the Locals (web, QUIC connection migration reaching a localhost-only service) and turbo-malloc (pwn, custom heap allocator), both with full writeups. I also built apex-ctf, a deliberately vulnerable web lab.
  • blueXploit — reproduction of Marc Newlin's Bluetooth CVE-2023-45866, with a payload runner and reconnection logic. 16 stars on GitHub.
